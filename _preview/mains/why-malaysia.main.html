The details that
make a difference.

Facilities and arrangements vary.
We help you ask the right questions.

Will language assistance be available?+

Ask the hospital which languages it supports and whether an interpreter needs to be arranged in advance.

Can I stay near my hospital?+

Discuss your preferred room type, family needs, accessibility and transport. Availability and rates are confirmed for your dates.

Is Malaysia the right option for every patient?+

The choice depends on the medical assessment, hospital suitability, budget and practical circumstances. Discuss your options with the treating team.

How can MediConnect help before I choose?+

We coordinate hospital enquiries and help clarify appointments, estimates and travel arrangements. Clinical decisions remain with the hospital.
