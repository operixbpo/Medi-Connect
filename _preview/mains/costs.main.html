Understand the costs.
Before you confirm.

Separate hospital care, travel arrangements and coordination fees so you know what to ask and what you are paying for.

01

Hospital care

An estimate issued by the hospital is the starting point, not a guaranteed final bill.

  • Ask about consultations, tests, procedures and room charges.
  • Check exclusions, currency and the estimate date.
  • Understand what could change after clinical assessment.
02

Travel & stay

Consider the whole visit, including the family member travelling with you.

  • Flights and visa-related expenses.
  • Accommodation, airport transfer and local transport.
  • Extra nights or changes to your itinerary.
03

Our coordination

Know what support is included before agreeing to arrangements.

  • A clear description of the services being arranged.
  • Any coordination fees and the payment recipient.
  • Cancellation or change terms for each arrangement.
