DESIGN REVIEW / VERSION 03

A preview of
what comes next.

This website demonstrates the proposed MediConnect experience. It is not yet an operational appointment or enquiry service.

What you can try

Explore all pages, filter hospitals, change the patient journey stage, check the travel preparation list and preview the guided enquiry and callback forms. Enter sample details only. Forms do not transmit or store information, and chat is not staffed.

Images and information

The skyline, care, equipment, accommodation and arrival scenes are AI-generated illustrations. They do not depict actual patients, staff, treatment outcomes, a named hospital or bookable accommodation. Hospital card artwork is conceptual. The approved MediConnect logo is supplied brand artwork.

Hospital descriptions draw on the official websites linked on each profile. No exclusive partnership, hospital representation or government endorsement is claimed. Clinical suitability, appointments, services and estimates must be confirmed by the hospital.

Before the service launches

We will add confirmed company and team details, exact office contacts, approved hospital imagery and relationship descriptions, applicable fees, privacy information and service terms. Calls, WhatsApp, forms and chat need to be connected and tested.

Design direction

A Liquid Glass-inspired navigation layer sits above clear, readable content. Pearl-white surfaces, medical blue and soft conceptual architecture bring continuity to the care journey.
